# NovaTel Tariff Catalogue (2026)

## Contract types

### Month-to-month
No commitment; the customer can cancel at any time without a fee. List prices apply
with no discount. Historically this is the segment with the highest churn: roughly
four in ten month-to-month customers leave, compared with about one in ten on one-year
and fewer than one in thirty on two-year contracts.

### One-year contract
12-month commitment with a 10% discount on the monthly base fee. Early termination
fee: the remaining months x 25% of the monthly fee. Customers may upgrade to a two-year
contract at any time without penalty.

### Two-year contract
24-month commitment with a 20% discount on the monthly base fee, plus one free
add-on service (Online Security or Tech Support) for the whole term. Early termination
fee: the remaining months x 25% of the monthly fee.

## Internet plans

| Plan | Technology | Speed | List price / month |
|------|-----------|-------|--------------------|
| Basic DSL | DSL | 24 Mbps | $29 |
| Fiber 300 | Fiber optic | 300 Mbps | $65 |
| Fiber 1000 | Fiber optic | 1 Gbps | $85 |

Fiber customers pay considerably more than DSL customers. Fiber customers on
month-to-month contracts without add-on services are a known high-risk segment.

## Phone plans

- **Single line**: $20/month, unlimited national calls.
- **Multiple lines**: $20 for the first line and $10 for each additional line.

## Add-on services ($5–10/month each)

- **Online Security**: network-level malware and phishing protection.
- **Online Backup**: 1 TB of cloud backup.
- **Device Protection**: repair or replacement of covered home devices.
- **Tech Support**: priority 24/7 support line with a 15-minute callback guarantee.
- **Streaming TV** and **Streaming Movies**: content bundles delivered over the internet line.

Customers with more add-on services are more tied into the NovaTel ecosystem and
churn less often. Customers with none of Online Security, Tech Support, Online Backup
or Device Protection churn noticeably more.
