# Active Retention Campaigns (Q3–Q4 2026)

Only customer care agents in the Retention team may apply these offers. Each offer can
be applied once per customer per 12 months unless stated otherwise. Record the campaign
code in the CRM.

## RET-LOCK24: Upgrade to two-year contract
- **Eligible:** month-to-month or one-year customers with tenure of at least 3 months.
- **Offer:** 25% off the monthly fee for 24 months (instead of the standard 20%), plus
  Tech Support free for the whole term.
- **Best for:** high-risk month-to-month customers who are satisfied with service
  quality but sensitive to price.

## RET-LOCK12: Upgrade to one-year contract
- **Eligible:** month-to-month customers.
- **Offer:** 15% off the monthly fee for 12 months.
- **Best for:** customers who refuse a two-year commitment.

## RET-SECURE: Free security & support bundle
- **Eligible:** internet customers without Online Security or Tech Support.
- **Offer:** Online Security and Tech Support free for 6 months, then 50% off for another
  6 months.
- **Best for:** customers whose risk is driven by missing add-on services, especially
  Fiber customers reporting connection or security issues.

## RET-AUTOPAY: Automatic payment incentive
- **Eligible:** customers paying by electronic check or mailed check.
- **Offer:** a $5/month credit for 12 months when they switch to automatic bank transfer
  or credit card.
- **Best for:** customers who pay by electronic check (the payment method with the highest
  churn rate) or who have billing complaints.

## RET-NEWBIE: First-year care package
- **Eligible:** tenure of 12 months or less.
- **Offer:** a proactive welcome call, one month free, and a free speed upgrade for 3
  months.
- **Best for:** new customers. Churn is concentrated in the first year of tenure.

## RET-FIBERVALUE: Fiber price match
- **Eligible:** Fiber optic customers with a monthly charge above $80 who received a
  competitor offer.
- **Offer:** a $15/month discount for 12 months. It requires a one-year contract.
- **Approval:** Team Lead approval is required when combined with any other campaign.

## Stacking rules
- At most **two** campaigns can be combined for one customer.
- RET-LOCK24 and RET-LOCK12 cannot be combined.
- The total discount must not exceed **35%** of the monthly fee without Retention
  Manager approval.
