# Technical Support: Common Issues

## Slow or unstable Fiber connection
1. Check for an active outage in the Network Status dashboard (by postal code).
2. Ask the customer to restart the ONT and router (unplug for 30 seconds).
3. Run a remote line test from the support console. Signal below -27 dBm indicates a
   fibre fault: book a technician visit (free of charge).
4. If Wi-Fi is the bottleneck, offer a mesh extender ($4/month, free with Tech Support).

Repeated connectivity complaints (3 or more in 90 days) are a strong churn signal. Flag
the account for the Retention team and consider RET-SECURE, which includes Tech Support.

## DSL speed lower than advertised
DSL speed depends on distance from the street cabinet. If the line test shows a sync
rate below 60% of the plan speed, check Fiber availability at the address and
offer a Fiber upgrade with the first month free.

## Streaming TV buffering
Streaming TV requires at least 25 Mbps per 4K stream. Customers on Basic DSL with
Streaming TV and Streaming Movies often experience buffering, so recommend a Fiber upgrade.

## Service level targets
- Tech Support add-on customers: 15-minute callback, technician visit within 24 hours.
- Standard customers: callback within 4 hours, technician visit within 72 hours.
