# Billing & Payments FAQ

## What payment methods does NovaTel accept?
Automatic bank transfer, credit card (automatic), electronic check, and mailed check.
Automatic methods are recommended: they avoid late fees and qualify for the
RET-AUTOPAY credit.

## Why did my first bill look higher than expected?
The first bill contains a prorated charge for the partial first month plus the full
upcoming month, so it can be up to twice the usual monthly amount. From the second
bill onward, the amount equals the monthly plan price.

## What happens if a payment is late?
A $7 late fee is added 10 days after the due date. After 30 days, services are
suspended. A reconnection fee is not charged if the balance is settled within 60 days.

## Can a customer get a refund when cancelling?
Monthly fees are charged in advance and are not prorated on cancellation.
Contract customers pay the early termination fee described in the Tariff Catalogue.

## What is paperless billing?
Bills are sent by email and are available in the NovaTel app. Customers can switch
back to paper at no cost. Paper bills carry a $2/month fee for new sign-ups since 2025.

## How is TotalCharges calculated?
TotalCharges is the cumulative amount billed over the customer's whole tenure. New
customers who have not yet been billed have TotalCharges = 0.
